\textbf{Literature.} Although QED is abelian and so lacks the color structure of Section~\ref{sec:color-ordering-planarity}, the spinor-helicity and on-shell methods carry over directly. \autocite{Ozeren:2005mp} adapts the MHV (CSW) rules and the BCFW recursion to QED, computing $e^+ e^- \to n\gamma$ and benchmarking against traditional spinor techniques. \autocite{Badger:2010eq} introduces a dressed BCFW recursion tailored to QED, giving compact NMHV and NNMHV expressions for a fermion pair coupling to $n$ photons and reducing the factorial growth of complexity to merely exponential. The massive case is treated in \autocite{Ema:2024vww}, which constructs the four- and five-point massive QED amplitudes by on-shell recursion (see Section~\ref{sec:massive-offshell-bcfw}).
