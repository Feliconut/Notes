\lecture{3}{Tue 16 Jan 2024 13:27}{Category Theory, and Augmented Homology}

\subsubsection{Augmented Homology}

Recall: for $X$ a topological space, we can construct a chain complex 
 \[
	S_*(X) = \left( \ldots \to S_1(X) \to S_0(X) \to  \ldots \right) 
.\] 
We can also obtain homologies from this chain complex.

Define a new chain complex $\tilde S_*(X)$. Let
\[
	\tilde S_n(X) = \begin{cases}
		S_n(X) & n \neq -1 \\
		\mathbb{Z} & n = -1
	\end{cases}
.\] 
So $\tilde S_*(X)$ looks like
\[
	\tilde S_*(X) = \left( \ldots \to  S_1(X) \to S_0(X) \to \mathbb{Z} \to 0 \to \ldots \right) 
.\] 
The boundary map $\epsilon: S_0(X) \to \mathbb{Z}$ is defined by 
\begin{align*}
	\epsilon: S_0(X) &\longrightarrow \mathbb{Z} \\
	c_x^0 &\longmapsto \epsilon(c_x^0) = 1
.\end{align*}
Extend by linearity.

We also want to check that $\epsilon \circ d_1: S_1(X) \to  S_0(X) \to \mathbb{Z}$ is zero: for a 1-simplex $\sigma$,
\begin{align*}
	\varepsilon \circ d(\sigma)
	&= \varepsilon(\sigma|_{[1]} - \sigma|_{[0]}) \\
	&= \varepsilon\left(c^0_{\sigma(1)} - c^0_{\sigma(0)}\right) \\
	&= 0
.\end{align*}
The augmented homology of $X$ is
\[
	\tilde H_n(X) = H_n(\tilde S_*(X))
.\] 
For $n \ge 1$, $\tilde H_n(X) = H_n(X)$.
Exercise: $H_0(X) \cong \tilde H_0(X) \oplus \mathbb{Z}$.
