\subsection{Motivation for special relativity}

\begin{example}[Pion Lifetime]
	
Pion will decay according to \begin{align}
\pi^+ &= \mu^+ + v_M \\
\pi^- &= \mu^- + \overline{v}_M 
.\end{align} 
Pion has a average lifetime of $\SI{26}{\nano\second}$ at rest. 
\end{example}

\subsubsection{Time is no longer absolute}

If we make a pion moving in velocity of \SI{2.737e8}{\meter\per\second}, the lifetime is measured by the stationary observer to be \SI{63.7}{\nano\second}. 

Prediction of classical theory: both should measure \SI{26}{\nano\second}.
