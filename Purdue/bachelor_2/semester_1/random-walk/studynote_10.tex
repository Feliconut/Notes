The following result is given.
\begin{theorem}(Theorem~2.3 of previous reference)
	Let $\varepsilon>0$. Consider sequence of BLP $Z^n$, with initial conditions $Z^n_0 = z_n$, where $z_n / n \to y > \varepsilon$.

	We have convergence in distribution in the Skorohod topology on cadlag functions on $[0,\infty )$:
	\[
		Y^{\varepsilon, n}(t) := \frac{Z^n_{\left\lfloor nt  \right\rfloor \wedge \sigma_{\varepsilon n}^{Z^n}}}{n}
		\Rightarrow
		Y(t \wedge \sigma_\varepsilon^Y)
	.\] 
	where $Y$ solves the SDE
	\[
		dY (t) = b_Z d t + \sqrt{ \nu Y(t)}  d B(t), \qquad Y(0) = y
	.\] 
\end{theorem}
