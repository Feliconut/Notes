\subsection*{Review 1}

The paper in question lacks proper attention to detail and fails to provide a comprehensive description of the experimental apparatus and procedures. It is unclear how the variables and parameters were measured, and the summary provided is vague and poorly organized. The lack of clarity and detail makes it difficult to replicate the experiment and calls into question the validity of the reported results.

Furthermore, the paper lacks proper references for the specialized equipment used, making it difficult for readers to procure the same equipment and replicate the experiment. The absence of any mention of design schematics or tables of parts only adds to the confusion and difficulty in understanding the experimental setup.

The analysis and interpretation of the results are also inadequate. The sources of error are briefly mentioned but not discussed in detail, leaving the reader wondering about the potential impact on the results. The discussion of the implications of the results lacks depth and originality, with no proposed plausible consequences for the theory or suggestions for improvements for future experiments.

Overall, this paper falls short in providing a clear and detailed description of the experimental apparatus and procedures, making it difficult to assess the validity of the reported results. The lack of references for specialized equipment, design schematics or tables of parts, and the inadequate analysis and interpretation of results make it difficult to replicate the experiment and limit the usefulness of this paper.
